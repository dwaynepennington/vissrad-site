VRSmartPlan Cost Book - data notice

The Cost Book data sets in this folder are built by tools/build_costbook.py from:

1. United States Construction Cost Database (USCCDB) v1.0
   Oga, T. (2026). https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22979157
   Compilation licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0),
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   Changes made: rows filtered to public values (public_domain and published_open_terms),
   paired (base and fringe, operating and standby rates), regrouped by state and region,
   and notes rewritten in English.
   Values from U.S. federal sources (DOL Davis-Bacon, BLS OEWS/PPI/ECI/CPI, USACE EP 1110-1-8
   and CWCCIS, FEMA, FHWA, FTA, DoD UFC 3-701-01, Census) are works of the U.S. Government,
   public domain in the United States (17 U.S.C. 105).
   NJDOT rows are reused under the open terms quoted in the USCCDB source ledger.

2. State and federal publications collected for VRSmartPlan:
   Caltrans Labor Surcharge and Equipment Rental Rates (2026-09); NCDOT 2025 Division Let
   Bid Averages; Mississippi DOT LPA Production Parameters (state publications, no stated
   reuse restriction); EIA AEO Electricity Market Module Table 3 and DoD UFC 3-701-01
   Tables 4-2 to 4-4 (U.S. federal, public domain).

Not included: values the DoD takes from commercial cost books (RSMeans, Marshall & Swift,
CostWorks, the TRACES/MII Cost Book behind UFC 3-701-01 Table 6); Texas DOT and Florida
DOT data; personal-use-only sources.

Rates are references for estimating. Check each one against the current source before
using it in a bid.
