Estimating

VissRadEST

Discipline takeoff and cost estimating for industrial piping, equipment, engineering and construction scope — the workbook discipline this whole suite grew out of. Every discipline carries three possible methods, and only the one you select feeds the rollup, so the basis of the number is never in doubt. The budget is handed forward to PCS and Cost rather than retyped.

3 methods per disciplineAACE class & accuracyBenchmark cross-checkCode of accountsIssue gatePDF takeoff

Methods

Parametric, known quantity and detailed takeoff sit side by side per discipline. Only the active method contributes direct cost, man-hours, labour and material; selecting detailed takeoff is refused if no takeoff lines exist.

Takeoff

Line-level quantities against library keys, with discipline, global and item factors, weld factors and craft assignment, priced at all-in craft rates.

Library

Man-hour library, weld factors, craft rates and material prices, each row carrying its source. Values generated for the demonstration are labelled as such.

Indirects

A code-of-accounts build-up from direct cost to total installed cost — EPCM, field indirects, owner cost, escalation and contingency, shown as a waterfall.

Engineering

FEED and detail phase man-hours, or a factored EPCM percentage. A double-count guard keeps only one engineering source live at a time.

Benchmark

An independent man-hour-per-USD-1,000 intensity cross-check against the built-up hours, with variance beyond ±20 per cent flagged.

Proposal

Estimate class, accuracy range, pricing basis and the fee to produce the estimate, with status moving Draft to In Review to Issued.

QA gate & audit

An estimate cannot be marked Issued while quality checks are open, and every method switch, line edit and status change is written to the audit trail.

PDF takeoff

Count, length and area measured on the drawing itself after setting its scale.

Benchmark intensities and library man-hours shown in the demonstration were generated for it and are not drawn from any proprietary man-hour manual.

Turnaround estimating

VissRadTAR

Turnaround scope is estimated one item at a time from a module library. Each module asks its own short set of questions — the size of the exchanger, whether the bundle is pulled, how many tubes are plugged — and the answers switch tasks on and off and drive the formulas for duration, crew, equipment and material. Every scope item on the worklist keeps its answers, so the whole event can be re-priced by changing a rate or an answer rather than by re-keying an estimate.

330 modulesWizardFragnetsMaterial take-offP6 XER, MPX, SAP PM

Module wizard

Numbered scope questions and sizing variables are asked one at a time, and only the questions that still matter are shown. A live estimate follows every answer before the item is added to the worklist.

Fragnet per scope item

Tasks carry logic, so each item produces a fragnet with its critical path. The worklist rolls onto the turnaround clock from feed-out, with the driving item and the window shown against each other. Every item sits in one of 187 standard refinery and chemical plant units, from crude, conversion and sulfur recovery to olefins, polymers, utilities and offsites, and the unit becomes its P6 WBS node.

Material estimating

Modules carry material quantity formulas priced from a site price book with waste allowances. Quoted or owner-furnished items are added per scope item, and the event material take-off is grouped by item with tax and freight.

Crews and manpower

Crafts and equipment are held in a resource manager with rates and a site productivity factor. The event produces a manpower histogram by shift and a resource summary by craft.

Module Editor

Questions, tasks, crews, logic and material lines are authored and validated in the browser, test-run against default answers and saved to the library. Estimating-tool Excel exports can be imported as a starting point.

Exports

Estimate and event workbooks, CSV, Primavera P6 XER, MPX, SAP PM operation lists, the material take-off and a printable field worksheet per scope item.

No sign-in. The library, worklist and price book are held in the browser on the computer running it.

Construction planning and execution

VissRadConstruction

The step between an estimate and a schedule. Work packages are broken into worksteps from commodity templates — quantity times the template’s productivity rate gives the budget hours, crew size gives the duration — and everything the plan names is checked against the target Primavera P6 project as it was exported before a single row is written. What leaves is a plan P6 can take on the first attempt, in whichever form the deployment actually allows.

3 deployment modesP6 XLSX, XER, XMLRule of creditPre-flight gateField sheetsPhoto albums

Deployment mode

REST where the database exposes it, a watched folder on a network where the Job Service is installed, or a spreadsheet imported by hand. The mode is chosen on entry and decides which write routes are offered, rather than promising a route the site cannot use.

Field execution

Daily construction reports with labour, equipment, weather and delays, crew timesheets, the safety log and permits to work with the job safety analysis. An RFI can be raised from a daily report.

Worksteps from templates

Ten commodity templates carry the productivity rate, crew mix, rule-of-credit steps, calendar, WBS node and cost account. The planner sets how much work and how many crews, not the method.

Pre-flight gate

Every calendar, resource, cost account, WBS node and activity code value named in the plan is checked against the target project. A spreadsheet import will otherwise succeed quietly with the assignment missing, which is the failure nobody notices until the hours do not appear.

Work already done

Scope that was built before it was planned can be typed in with the plan — installed quantity and hours to date, with the dates it happened. Credit is derived from quantity or from the rule-of-credit steps, never accepted as a typed percentage.

Field sheets and returns

Sheets are issued by work package and come back as installed quantity and completed steps. The return carries who reported it and when it was captured, so a number can be traced to a sheet.

Writing to P6

XLSX to the published Oracle import field names, XER, P6 XML, CSV, Job Service actions XML, or a REST preview. The activity’s percent complete type decides which field carries progress — physical, units or remaining duration — because P6 calculates the rest.

Photos

Tagged construction photos grouped into albums by area, date or activity, with the time taken and GPS position where the file carries it.

Two passes, in order: the plan load creates the activities, the progress write statuses them. P6 cannot status an activity that does not exist yet, so the gate holds progress until the plan has been loaded.

Schedule development, analysis and maintenance alongside the Primavera P6 data the rest of the suite consumes, keeping activity structure aligned to the work breakdown structure that progress is measured against. Resource levelling

Limits from the file’s resource rates or typed in, with before and after histograms and the activities delayed. A serial heuristic: it will not reproduce P6’s levelling exactly.

Exchange layer

VissRad Link

Link is not an application at all. It is the outbox, inbox, document schema and transports that make the applications behave as one system — a certified quantity leaving PCS arrives in Cost as the same document, with the same identity, or it fails loudly and waits.

11 document typesIdempotent resendRetry ladderFull replayHub console

Dashboard

Queue depth, in-flight, acked and failed counts across every flow, with the last error visible rather than buried.

Outbox and inbox

Every document with its type, route, idempotency key, attempt count and status, on both sides of the exchange.

Flows

Which application sends what to which, as a declared route rather than a convention someone remembers.

Transports

File drop, shared folder or HTTP endpoint per route, so the same document schema works on an air-gapped plant network or across sites.

Settings and replay

Retry ladder, idempotent resend and full replay from the hub console, so a failed transfer is repaired rather than retyped.

Audit

The transfer history itself — what was sent, when, by which attempt, and what the receiving application did with it.
